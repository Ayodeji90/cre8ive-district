# Cre8ive District research programme

This folder contains six parallel research tracks requested by the founder. Each report should distinguish sourced observations from recommendations and open questions, cover Nigeria and international operations where relevant, and separate launch requirements from later expansion.

1. Website engineering requirements and architecture
2. Competitor landscape and possible market gaps
3. Operating strategy and service delivery workflows
4. Organization design, roles, skills, and hiring sequence
5. Customer acquisition strategy
6. Social media strategy and operating workflow

The six reports are synthesized in [00-synthesis-and-roadmap.md](00-synthesis-and-roadmap.md). No competitor claim, price, headcount, or market gap should be treated as validated solely because it appears in a public website or desk-research report.
