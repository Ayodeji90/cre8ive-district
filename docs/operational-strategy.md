### 1.2 Lean Team Structure (3-8 People)

#### Phase 1: Founding Team (3 people)

| Role | Covers | Person |
|---|---|---|
| **Creative Director / Founder** | Branding strategy, client relations, business development, QA on all deliverables | 1 |

| **Digital Marketer / Content Creator** | Social media management, ad campaigns, copywriting, content production | 1 |

| Role | Focus |
|---|---|
| Creative Director / Founder | Strategy, partnerships, high-value clients |
| Lead Designer | Brand identity, complex design projects |
| Junior Designer | Flyers, social media graphics, production work |
| Senior Web Developer | Architecture, complex builds, mobile apps |

| Digital Marketing Lead | Ad strategy, client reporting, budget management |
| Content Creator | Video, photography, social content |
| Project / Operations Manager | Client communication, scheduling, invoicing, workflow |

### 1.3 Role Descriptions

**Creative Director / Founder**
- Sets creative direction for all projects
- Manages client relationships for key accounts
- Reviews and approves all deliverables before client presentation
- Handles business development, pricing, and partnerships
- Makes hiring decisions and sets team priorities

**Lead Designer**
- Owns the branding process from brief to final delivery
- Creates logo concepts, brand identity systems, and style guides
- Designs print materials (flyers, banners, business cards, packaging)
- Prepares print-ready files and coordinates with printing vendors
- Mentors junior designers on brand consistency

**Web Developer**
- Builds responsive websites (custom HTML/CSS/JS, React, WordPress, Shopify)
- Develops mobile applications (React Native or Flutter)
- Sets up e-commerce stores with payment integration (Paystack, Flutterwave)
- Handles domain registration, hosting setup, and deployment
- Provides post-launch technical support and maintenance

**Digital Marketing Lead**
- Develops advertising strategy per client (platform selection, audience targeting, budget allocation)
- Sets up and manages campaigns across Meta, TikTok, and Google
- Monitors campaign performance daily, optimizes spend
- Produces monthly performance reports with actionable insights
- Manages ad creative production pipeline with the design team

**Content Creator / Social Media Manager**
- Creates social media content (graphics, short videos, reels, carousels)
- Writes captions and copy for posts and ads
- Manages content calendars and scheduling
- Engages with audience (comments, DMs, community management)
- Tracks social media analytics and reports on growth

**Project / Operations Manager** (hire when monthly projects exceed 8)
- First point of contact after sales handoff
- Manages project timelines, assigns tasks, tracks progress
- Handles invoicing, payment follow-ups, and vendor coordination
- Maintains client communication throughout project lifecycle
- Ensures deliverables meet quality standards before client review

## 2. Client Onboarding Pipeline

### 2.1 Pipeline Overview

```
Inquiry → Qualification → Brief → Proposal → Payment → Kickoff → Delivery → Follow-up
```

### 2.2 Detailed Steps

#### Step 1: Inquiry (Day 0)

**Channels:** WhatsApp Business, Instagram DM, Email, Website contact form, Referral

**Action:**
- Respond within 2 hours during business hours (9am-6pm WAT)
- Use a standard greeting template:

> "Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out to Cre8ive District! We'd love to learn more about your project. Could you share:
> 1. What service you're interested in (branding, website, ads, or a combination)?
> 2. A brief description of your business?
> 3. Your timeline and budget range?
> We'll get back to you with next steps within 24 hours."

- Log the inquiry in your CRM / project management tool

#### Step 2: Qualification Call (Day 0-1)

**Action:**
- 15-minute WhatsApp or Google Meet call
- Determine: scope, budget fit, timeline, decision-maker
- Disqualify if: budget is below minimum thresholds, scope is outside expertise, timeline is unrealistic

**Minimum project thresholds:**

| Service | Minimum Budget |
|---|---|
| Logo only | ₦80,000 |
| Full brand identity | ₦250,000 |
| Basic website (5 pages) | ₦300,000 |
| Mobile app | ₦1,500,000 |
| Monthly ad management | ₦150,000/month (excl. ad spend) |
| Social media management | ₦100,000/month |

#### Step 3: Discovery Brief (Day 1-3)

**Action:**
- Send the client a structured brief form (Google Form or Notion form) covering:
  - Business name, industry, target audience
  - What they sell / offer
  - Competitors they admire (and why)
  - Existing brand assets (logo, colors, website)
  - Project goals and success criteria
  - Budget confirmation
  - Timeline expectations
- For larger projects (₦500K+), schedule a 45-minute discovery session instead

#### Step 4: Proposal & Quote (Day 3-5)

**Action:**
- Prepare a professional proposal document (PDF or Notion page) including:
  - Understanding of the client's needs (prove you listened)
  - Scope of work with clear deliverables
  - Timeline with milestones
  - Pricing breakdown
  - Payment terms
  - What's included and what's not
  - Terms and conditions (revision limits, turnaround, ownership)
- Send via email with a follow-up WhatsApp message
- Proposal valid for 14 days

#### Step 5: Payment & Agreement (Day 5-7)

**Payment terms:**
- Projects under ₦200K: 100% upfront
- Projects ₦200K-₦500K: 50% upfront, 50% before final delivery
- Projects ₦500K-₦2M: 40% upfront, 30% at midpoint, 30% before delivery
- Projects above ₦2M: 30% upfront, then milestone-based
- Retainers (monthly): full month payment due before month starts

**Payment methods:**
- Bank transfer (primary — provide GTBank / Access Bank details)
- Paystack payment link (for card payments)
- Flutterwave invoice (for international clients)

**Action:**
- Send a simple one-page agreement / engagement letter
- Generate and send invoice via Paystack or manually
- Confirm payment receipt within 24 hours
- Add project to the active board

#### Step 6: Kickoff (Day 7-8)

**Action:**
- Internal: assign project lead, create project folder (Google Drive), set up project board (Notion/Trello)
- Client-facing: send a kickoff message confirming:
  - Project start date
  - Key milestones and dates
  - Who their point of contact is
  - What you need from them (assets, access, content)
  - Communication channel and response expectations

**Kickoff message template:**

> "Hi [Name], we're excited to kick off your [project type]! Here's what to expect:
>
> **Timeline:** [start] → [end]
> **Your contact:** [name + WhatsApp]
> **Next step:** [specific action] by [date]
>
> We'll share progress at each milestone. Questions? Just message us here."

#### Step 7: Delivery (varies by project)

See Section 3 for department-specific workflows.

#### Step 8: Post-Delivery Follow-up (Day 3-7 after delivery)

**Action:**
- Send a thank-you message
- Ask for a Google review or testimonial
- Share the project on Cre8ive District's social media (with permission)
- Offer a 10% discount on their next project
- Add to newsletter / client list for future promotions
- Schedule a check-in for 30 days later (especially for websites and brand launches)

### 2.3 Onboarding Timeline Summary

| Step | Timeline | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Inquiry response | Within 2 hours | Founder / Ops Manager |
| Qualification call | Within 24 hours | Founder |
| Brief / discovery | Day 1-3 | Founder + relevant lead |
| Proposal sent | Day 3-5 | Founder |
| Payment received | Day 5-7 | Ops Manager |
| Kickoff | Day 7-8 | Project lead |

**Target: Inquiry to kickoff in under 10 business days.**

## 3. Project Workflows by Department

### 3.1 Branding & Design Projects

#### Workflow

```
Brief → Research & Moodboard → Concepts → Client Review → Revisions → Final Files → Print Handoff
```

| Phase | Duration | Activities | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brief** | Day 1 | Review client brief, discuss vision, analyze competitors | Completed brief document |
| **Research & Moodboard** | Day 2-3 | Visual research, color exploration, typography options, moodboard creation | Moodboard (PDF or Figma link) — shared with client for alignment |
| **Concepts** | Day 4-7 | Develop 2-3 logo concepts, apply to mockups, prepare presentation | Concept presentation (PDF) with rationale for each direction |
| **Client Review** | Day 8-9 | Present concepts via video call or in-person, gather feedback | Written feedback / chosen direction |
| **Revisions** | Day 10-12 | Refine chosen concept, 2 rounds of revisions included | Revised designs |
| **Final Files** | Day 13-14 | Prepare all file formats, brand guidelines document | Logo pack (AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, JPG), brand guidelines PDF |
| **Print Handoff** | Day 15+ | Prepare print-ready files (CMYK, bleed, crop marks), coordinate with printer | Print-ready PDFs, vendor coordination |

**Standard turnaround:**
- Logo only: 7-10 business days
- Full brand identity: 14-21 business days
- Flyer / single print item: 3-5 business days
- Business card design: 2-3 business days

**Revision policy:**
- 2 rounds of revisions included in quoted price
- Additional revisions billed at ₦15,000 per round
- Major direction changes (starting over) are treated as a new project

#### Deliverables Checklist — Full Brand Identity

- [ ] Primary logo (full color, black, white, monochrome)
- [ ] Logo variations (horizontal, stacked, icon-only)
- [ ] Color palette (primary, secondary, accent — with HEX, RGB, CMYK, Pantone)
- [ ] Typography selections (heading font, body font, where to get them)
- [ ] Brand guidelines document (PDF, 8-15 pages)
- [ ] Social media templates (profile picture, cover photo, post template)
- [ ] Business card design (front and back)
- [ ] Letterhead design
- [ ] Email signature template
- [ ] File package organized in labeled folders

### 3.2 Web & App Development Projects

#### Workflow

```
Discovery → Sitemap & Wireframes → UI Design → Development → Testing → Launch → Maintenance
```

| Phase | Duration | Activities | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Discovery** | Week 1 | Requirements gathering, content audit, competitor analysis, technical planning | Requirements document, sitemap |
| **Wireframes** | Week 1-2 | Low-fidelity wireframes for key pages, user flow mapping | Wireframes (Figma) — client approval required |
| **UI Design** | Week 2-3 | High-fidelity mockups, responsive variants (desktop + mobile), design system | Design mockups (Figma) — client approval required |
| **Development** | Week 3-6 | Front-end build, back-end integration, CMS setup, payment integration | Staging site URL |
| **Content Population** | Week 5-6 | Add real content, images, copy (client provides or we create) | Content-complete staging site |
| **Testing** | Week 6-7 | Cross-browser testing, mobile testing, performance optimization, payment testing | QA report, bug fixes |
| **Launch** | Week 7-8 | Domain pointing, SSL setup, go-live, post-launch monitoring | Live website, login credentials |
| **Maintenance** | Ongoing | Monthly updates, security patches, backups, minor edits | Monthly maintenance report |

**Standard turnaround:**
- Landing page (1 page): 1-2 weeks
- Business website (5-8 pages): 4-6 weeks
- E-commerce store: 6-10 weeks
- Mobile app (MVP): 10-16 weeks
- Custom web application: 12-20 weeks

**Tech stack recommendations:**

| Project Type | Recommended Stack |
|---|---|
| Simple business site | HTML/CSS/JS or WordPress + Elementor |
| Portfolio / agency site | Next.js or Astro (static) |
| E-commerce (small) | Shopify or WooCommerce |
| E-commerce (custom) | Next.js + Paystack/Flutterwave |
| Mobile app | React Native or Flutter |
| Web application | Next.js + Node.js + PostgreSQL |

**Client responsibilities:**
- Provide all text content (or pay for copywriting add-on)
- Provide high-quality images (or pay for stock/photography)
- Provide timely feedback at each approval gate (48-hour response expected)
- Provide domain and hosting credentials (or we purchase on their behalf)

#### Approval Gates (Hard stops — no progress without sign-off)

1. **Wireframe approval** — must be signed off before design begins
2. **Design approval** — must be signed off before development begins
3. **Content approval** — must be confirmed before launch
4. **Pre-launch approval** — final client walkthrough before going live

### 3.3 Promotion & Ads Projects

#### Workflow

```
Audit → Strategy → Creative Production → Campaign Setup → Launch → Optimization → Reporting
```

| Phase | Duration | Activities | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Audit** | Day 1-3 | Review existing social media, past ad performance, website/funnel, competitors | Audit document with findings and opportunities |
| **Strategy** | Day 3-5 | Define objectives (awareness, leads, sales), audience targeting, platform selection, budget allocation, KPIs | Ad strategy document |
| **Creative Production** | Day 5-10 | Design ad graphics/videos, write ad copy, create landing pages if needed | Ad creatives (3-5 variations per campaign), copy document |
| **Campaign Setup** | Day 10-12 | Set up ad accounts, create campaigns in platform, configure pixels/tracking, set budgets | Live campaigns (paused, ready to launch) |
| **Launch** | Day 12 | Activate campaigns, verify tracking, confirm delivery | Running campaigns |
| **Optimization** | Ongoing (daily) | Monitor performance, adjust targeting, pause underperformers, scale winners, A/B test | Weekly performance snapshots |
| **Reporting** | Monthly | Compile results, analyze against KPIs, provide recommendations for next month | Monthly report (PDF or Notion) |

**Platform-Specific Approach:**

| Platform | Best For | Typical Budget Range (Monthly Ad Spend) | Nigerian Market Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)** | E-commerce, service businesses, lead gen, brand awareness | ₦50,000 - ₦2,000,000 | Most mature platform in Nigeria. Best for broad reach. Instagram stories/reels perform well for younger demo. |
| **TikTok Ads** | Youth-focused brands, fashion, food, entertainment, app installs | ₦100,000 - ₦1,000,000 | Growing rapidly in Nigeria. Lower CPMs than Meta. Best for viral/trend-based content. Requires video-first creative. |
| **Google Ads** | Service businesses, B2B, high-intent searches, local businesses | ₦100,000 - ₦1,500,000 | Search ads work well for services people actively look for (e.g., "branding agency Lagos"). Display/YouTube for awareness. |
| **Twitter/X Ads** | Tech, media, news, opinion leaders | ₦50,000 - ₦500,000 | Niche but effective for tech startups and media brands in Nigeria. |

**Management fee structure:**

| Monthly Ad Spend | Management Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to ₦200,000 | ₦100,000 flat |
| ₦200,000 - ₦500,000 | ₦150,000 flat |
| ₦500,000 - ₦1,000,000 | 20% of ad spend |
| ₦1,000,000+ | 15% of ad spend |

**Monthly reporting template includes:**
- Total spend vs. budget
- Impressions, reach, clicks, CTR
- Cost per click (CPC), cost per lead (CPL), cost per acquisition (CPA)
- Conversions / leads generated
- Return on ad spend (ROAS) where applicable
- Top-performing creatives
- Recommendations for next month

## 5. Pricing Framework

### 5.1 Pricing Models

| Model | Best For | How It Works |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed Project** | Branding, website builds, campaign launches | Quoted price for defined scope. Client knows total cost upfront. |
| **Monthly Retainer** | Social media management, ad management, maintenance | Fixed monthly fee for ongoing services. Predictable revenue. |
| **Hourly** | Consulting, ad-hoc design tasks, overflow work | Billed per hour. Track time carefully. Use for undefined scopes. |

**Default recommendation:** Fixed project pricing for most work. Retainers for ongoing services. Avoid hourly unless the client specifically requests it (harder to predict cost, creates friction).

## 6. Quality Control & Delivery

### 6.1 Internal Review Checkpoints

Every deliverable passes through at least two checkpoints before the client sees it:

| Checkpoint | Who Reviews | What They Check |
|---|---|---|
| **Self-review** | Creator | Completeness, spelling, alignment, file quality |
| **Peer review** | Another team member | Fresh-eyes check for errors, brand consistency, UX issues |
| **Lead review** | Department lead or Creative Director | Strategic alignment, quality standard, client-readiness |

For high-value projects (₦500K+), the Creative Director personally reviews before any client presentation.

### 6.2 Client Approval Gates

| Gate | When | Format | What Happens Next |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brief sign-off** | After discovery | WhatsApp/email confirmation | Work begins |
| **Moodboard / direction approval** | After research phase | PDF or Figma link | Concept development starts |
| **Concept / design approval** | After design phase | Presentation call or PDF | Revisions or development |
| **Revision approval** | After each revision round | WhatsApp/email | Next revision or final files |
| **Final approval** | Before delivery / launch | Written "approved" via email | Final files prepared / site goes live |

**Rule: Never proceed past a gate without written client approval.** A WhatsApp "Looks good, go ahead" counts — screenshot and save it.

### 6.3 File Delivery Standards

#### Branding Deliverables

| File Type | Format | Color Mode | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Logo (vector) | .AI, .EPS, .SVG | RGB + CMYK versions | Vector (scalable) |
| Logo (raster) | .PNG (transparent bg), .JPG | RGB | 300 DPI minimum |
| Print files | .PDF (press-ready) | CMYK | 300 DPI, 3mm bleed |
| Social media | .PNG or .JPG | RGB | Platform-specific dimensions |
| Brand guidelines | .PDF | RGB | 150 DPI (screen-optimized) |

**Folder structure for brand delivery:**

```
[Client Name] - Brand Package/
├── Logo/
│   ├── Primary/
│   │   ├── Full-Color/  (AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, JPG)
│   │   ├── Black/
│   │   ├── White/
```

## 7. Growth & Scaling Plan

### 7.1 Stage 1: Solo Founder / Side Hustle (₦0 - ₦300K/month revenue)

**Duration:** Month 1-6

**Focus:**
- Build portfolio (do 3-5 projects at discounted rates or pro bono for strong brands)
- Establish social media presence (post daily on Instagram, weekly on LinkedIn)
- Set up brand identity for Cre8ive District itself
- Create a professional website (use this project)
- Handle everything personally: sales, design, delivery

**Key actions:**
- Register business name with CAC
- Open a business bank account
- Set up WhatsApp Business with catalog
- Build 5+ portfolio pieces
- Get 3+ testimonials / Google reviews

**Revenue target:** ₦200,000 - ₦300,000/month by month 6

### 7.2 Stage 2: First Hire (₦300K - ₦800K/month revenue)

**Duration:** Month 6-12

**Hire:** One junior designer or content creator (₦80,000 - ₦150,000/month)

**Why this hire first:** Frees the founder from production work so they can focus on sales and client relationships, which directly drive revenue.

**Focus:**
- Systematize your workflow (documented processes, templates, checklists)
- Raise prices 20-30% as portfolio grows
- Start offering monthly retainers (social media management)
- Begin running ads for your own business
- Attend networking events, join business communities (LCCI, tech meetups)

**Revenue target:** ₦600,000 - ₦800,000/month by month 12

### 7.3 Stage 3: Core Team (₦800K - ₦2M/month revenue)

**Duration:** Month 12-24

**Team:** 3-5 people

**Hiring priority:**
1. Web developer (₦150,000 - ₦300,000/month) — opens the highest-ticket service line
2. Digital marketer (₦120,000 - ₦200,000/month) — retainer revenue, recurring income
3. Promote junior designer to lead, hire another junior

**Focus:**
- Three departments now operational
- Start creating package bundles (brand + website + marketing)
- Build a referral program (10% commission to referrers)
- Consider a physical office or co-working space (₦50,000 - ₦150,000/month)
- Formalize contracts and terms of service with a lawyer

**Revenue target:** ₦1,500,000 - ₦2,000,000/month by month 24

### 7.4 Stage 4: Agency Scale (₦2M - ₦5M/month revenue)

**Duration:** Month 24-36

**Team:** 6-10 people

**Hiring priority:**
1. Operations / Project Manager — critical to maintain quality as project volume grows
2. Second developer (or senior developer to lead)
3. Content creator / videographer — video drives social and ads
4. Account manager (for 10+ active clients)

**Focus:**
- Position as a premium agency (raise prices, be selective with clients)
- Develop case studies (before/after, results achieved)
- Explore partnerships (printers, photographers, SaaS companies)
- Consider productized services (e.g., "Brand-in-a-Week" fixed scope/price)
- Build passive income streams (templates, courses, digital products)

**Revenue target:** ₦3,000,000 - ₦5,000,000/month by month 36

### 7.5 Key Scaling Triggers

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Turning down projects due to capacity | Time to hire |
| Founder spending >50% time on production | Hire production help, founder focuses on sales + strategy |
| Monthly projects exceed 8 | Hire an operations/project manager |
| Quality complaints increasing | Slow down, add review checkpoints, improve onboarding |
| Revenue plateaus for 3+ months | Invest in marketing, raise prices, add a service line |
| Ad management clients exceed 5 | Hire a dedicated digital marketer |

## 8. KPIs & Metrics

### 8.1 Business-Level KPIs

| Metric | Target | How to Track |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly revenue | Growth: 10-15% month-over-month in year 1 | Google Sheets / Wave |
| Monthly profit margin | 40-60% (services business) | Revenue minus salaries, tools, overhead |
| Active clients | 5-10 at any time (early stage) | Notion / project board |
| Client acquisition cost (CAC) | Below ₦30,000 per client | Total marketing spend ÷ new clients |
| Average project value | Increasing quarter-over-quarter | Total revenue ÷ number of projects |
| Repeat client rate | 30%+ | Returning clients ÷ total clients |
| Referral rate | 20%+ of new clients from referrals | Track source of each inquiry |

### 8.2 Branding & Design KPIs

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Average turnaround time | Logo: 7 days, Brand identity: 14 days | Track from kickoff to final delivery |
| Revision rounds per project | ≤ 2 (average) | More = brief was unclear or concepts were weak |
| Client satisfaction score | 4.5+ / 5 | Post-project survey (Google Form, 3 questions) |
| Portfolio pieces added/month | 2+ | Only count work you're proud to show |

### 8.3 Web & App Development KPIs

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| On-time delivery rate | 80%+ | Projects delivered by quoted deadline |
| Post-launch bug reports | < 3 critical in first month | Test thoroughly before launch |
| Website performance score | 80+ on Google PageSpeed Insights | Check before delivery |
| Maintenance client retention | 80%+ annual retention | Monthly maintenance converts to recurring revenue |

### 8.4 Promotion & Ads KPIs

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Client ROAS | 3x+ for e-commerce, varies for lead gen | Return on ad spend — the metric clients care about most |
| Cost per lead (CPL) | Depends on industry — track and benchmark | Lower is better; compare against industry averages |
| Click-through rate (CTR) | Meta: 1%+, Google Search: 3%+, TikTok: 0.5%+ | Below these = creative or targeting needs work |
| Client retention (monthly) | 6+ months average | If clients churn before month 3, diagnose why |
| Reporting on time | 100% — report delivered by the 5th of each month | Late reports erode trust |

### 8.5 Operational KPIs

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Inquiry-to-close rate | 25-35% | Of all inquiries, how many become paying clients |
| Inquiry response time | < 2 hours (business hours) | Speed of first response directly impacts conversion |
| Invoice collection time | < 7 days from invoice | Chase after 3 days, escalate after 7 |
| Team utilization rate | 70-80% | Billable hours ÷ available hours. Below 60% = underutilized. Above 85% = burnout risk. |

### 8.6 Monthly Review Cadence

**First Monday of each month — 1-hour team review:**

1. Revenue and profit review (15 min)
2. Project status updates — what's on track, what's delayed (15 min)
3. Client feedback and satisfaction scores (10 min)
4. Marketing/ads performance across client accounts (10 min)
5. What worked, what didn't, what to improve (10 min)

**Track everything in a simple Google Sheet dashboard.** Upgrade to a proper BI tool (e.g., Google Looker Studio) when the data volume justifies it.
