Common questions

How long does a branding project take?

A full brand identity (logo, guidelines, social templates) typically takes 2–3 weeks. Rush delivery is available for an additional fee.

How much does a website cost?

Our Business Website package starts at ₦400,000 for up to 5 pages. E-commerce, custom apps and larger sites are quoted separately based on scope.

Do you charge for ad spend?

No — our Ads Management fee covers strategy, creative and management. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta/Google by you and is separate from our fee.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer, Paystack, and Flutterwave. We require 50% upfront on all projects, with the balance due on delivery.

Do you work with clients outside Lagos?

Yes — we work with clients all over Nigeria and the diaspora. All communication and delivery is handled online. Printing can be shipped nationwide.

How many revisions do I get?

Most packages include 2 rounds of revisions. Additional revisions are billed at ₦15,000 per round. We're collaborative by nature so revisions are rarely needed.
