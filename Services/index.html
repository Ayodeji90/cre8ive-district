Questions

Before you pick
a plan.

If something is still unclear, send it over. We answer directly, including when a smaller plan would serve you better.

Which track am I on?

If you have no brand yet, or you are launching a new product or business, start with Track A. If customers already know your name and you want to change how they see it, start with Track B. The two tracks share the same process, the difference is research depth and rollout support.

Can I take one service instead of a whole plan?

Yes. Most single services are priced from the figures listed above: identity from ₦450,000, website from ₦600,000, campaign work from ₦250,000. If your need sits between two plans, we will quote it directly instead of forcing a package.

How long until we can start?

We usually begin two to three weeks after the deposit clears, and we only take three projects at a time. Rush timelines are possible for a 25% priority fee if the calendar allows it.

What do you need from us?

One decision maker, access to any existing brand files, and two hours in the first week for the kickoff session. After that, review points happen at the end of each stage rather than daily.

How does payment work?

Project plans are paid in three parts: 50% to book the slot, 30% once design is approved, 20% before handover. Monthly plans are billed on the same date each month. All prices are in Naira and exclusive of VAT.

What happens at handover?

You receive the source files, exported assets, editable templates and a written guide covering how to use the brand. For websites, we also record a short walkthrough of the build and leave you the keys, with no lock-in to us for future changes.
